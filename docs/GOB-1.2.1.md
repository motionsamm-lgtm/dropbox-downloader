# GOB 1.2.1 — Brick theme

- Brick brings violet download controls, crisp outlines, and light and dark palettes to Windows and macOS.
- New installations start with Brick and follow the system appearance. Existing installations keep their saved appearance. Switch to Brick under **Settings → Appearance → Theme**.
- Downloads, pause and resume, saved history, transfer settings, and in-app updates remain available.

Use **Check for updates** in GOB, or download the Windows installer, Windows portable ZIP, or Mac ZIP for your processor from this release.
