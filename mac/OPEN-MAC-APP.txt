Dropbox Downloader for Mac

Choose the ZIP for your Mac chip. The app requires macOS 14 or newer.
1. Extract the ZIP and open the folder.
2. Move Dropbox Downloader.app to Applications.
3. Open Dropbox Downloader from Applications.

Node.js and aria2 are included. The app is ad hoc signed for testing.
macOS may block the first launch because Apple hasn't notarized this build.
If you trust the person who gave you the build and decide to run it,
see Apple's instructions for allowing that specific app:
https://support.apple.com/102445

First test a small Dropbox file, then a large file you can pause and resume.
Use History and Open text log to see what happened.
